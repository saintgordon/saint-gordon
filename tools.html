TOOL / 001 LIVE · WEB AUDIO CONSOLE

Master Clash

Blind Mastering Shootout & A/B Comparison Console

Drop any batch of master renders or an entire folder into your browser and choose the strongest master strictly by ear. Master Clash runs permanently in Pure Blind Mode — sealing filenames, formats, and upload order until a winner is crowned — while silently matching perceived loudness so a louder bounce can never trick your ear.

  • Pure Blind Mode always on
  • ITU-R BS.1770 LUFS loudness matching
  • Sample-locked 0-latency A/B switching
  • Stereo / Mono / Side monitoring matrix
  • King of the Hill, Round-Robin & ABX modes
  • Selectable waveform loop & Markdown report

SIGNAL CHAIN // PURE BLIND LOCAL ONLY
  1. 01 / DROP Load multiple masters

    Automatically filters for MASTER / MASTERED and excludes UNMASTERED bounces. Identities stay sealed immediately.

  2. 02 / COMPARE Switch cleanly on the beat

    Pick your chorus or midpoint on the waveform, check Mono & Side, jot blind notes, and vote with keyboard shortcuts.

  3. 03 / VERDICT Break the seal & export

    Reveals the champion filename, integrated LUFS, peak dBFS, crest factor, full rankings, and an exportable Markdown report.

TOOL / 002 LIVE · RELEASE CHECKER

Pre-Flight

Plain-English Release Readiness & Streaming Loudness Checker

Drop your finished master before uploading to Spotify, Apple Music, YouTube, or TikTok. Pre-Flight checks loudness, 4× oversampled true peak, clipping, mono fold-down, and bass phase — then gives you numbered, plain-English DAW fix steps and a live A/B player so you can hear exact platform turn-down before releasing.

  • Plain-English DAW fix steps
  • LUFS, 4× True Peak & LRA metering
  • Spotify, Apple, YouTube & TikTok A/B
  • Clipping & inter-sample peak finder
  • Phone / Mono & <120 Hz bass phase check
  • Exportable Markdown audit report

  1. 01 / DROP Inspect your master in seconds

TOOL / 004 LIVE · METADATA & ARTWORK STUDIO

Tag & Seal

Lossless Audio Metadata Editor & Cover Art Embedder

Embed cover art and edit key track metadata directly inside WAV, MP3, and FLAC files without re-encoding a single audio sample. Tag & Seal writes universally compatible RIFF INFO + ID3v2.3 and FLAC Picture blocks so your artwork, title, artist, BPM, key, and ISRC show up cleanly across Finder, Apple Music, DJ decks, and phones.

  • Lossless WAV, MP3 & FLAC container editing
  • Zero audio re-encoding (bit-for-bit verified)
  • Cover art studio + auto JPEG/PNG convert
  • 1-click Xerox cover art filter option
  • Title, Artist, BPM, Key/Camelot, ISRC & lyrics
  • Preserves BEXT, cue & studio chunks

CONTAINER // BIT-EXACT SEAL LOCAL ONLY

TOOL / 007 LIVE · PRE-MIX STEM VALIDATOR

Stem Check

Pre-Mix Stem Validator — Paired with Saint Gordon Collaborate

Drop your beat and vocal stems into your browser before sending them for mixing. Stem Check inspects raw file headers to catch 44.1 kHz vs. 48 kHz mismatches, checks that every stem starts cleanly at 0:00 with matching length, flags digital clipping, and runs a Mid/Side check to catch baked-in stereo reverb or effects on dry mono vocals.

  • 0:00 start & timeline length alignment
  • True native 44.1k vs. 48k header check
  • Digital clipping (0 dBFS) burst scanner
  • Mid/Side baked-in stereo FX detector
  • Stacked multi-track sync playback + Solo Side
  • Clear Pass / Fix checklist & .md report
